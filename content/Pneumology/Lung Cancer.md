> [!abstract] Lung Cancer
> Malignant neoplasm arising from the respiratory epithelium (bronchi, bronchioles, and alveoli), classified clinically into **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC; ~85%)**—comprising **Adenocarcinoma** (~40–50%), **Squamous Cell Carcinoma** (~25%), and **Large Cell Carcinoma** (<10%)—and **Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC; ~15%)**, a high-grade neuroendocrine tumor with rapid doubling time and early metastasis.

> [!note] Pathophysiology
>
> - Cumulative carcinogen exposure (**cigarette smoke [85–90%]**, **radon [#2 overall, #1 in non-smokers]**, asbestos, arsenic, chromium, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons) or driver mutations (**EGFR, ALK, ROS1, KRAS G12C, BRAF V600E**) causes field cancerization, dysplasia, and malignant transformation.
> - **Central tumors (SCLC, Squamous):** Cause endobronchial obstruction (post-obstructive pneumonia, hemoptysis) and mediastinal invasion (**SVC syndrome**, recurrent laryngeal/phrenic nerve palsy).
> - **Peripheral tumors (Adenocarcinoma, Large Cell):** Invade pleura (**malignant effusion**) or chest apex (**Pancoast tumor**).

> [!Tip]- Clinical PEARLS
>
> - **Annual Low-Dose CT (LDCT) Screening Criteria (USPSTF):** Adults aged **50–80 years** with a **$\ge 20$ pack-year smoking history** who currently smoke or have **quit within the past 15 years** (reduces lung cancer mortality by 20%).
> - **Biopsy the Most Distant Metastatic Site First:** To simultaneously establish **histologic diagnosis AND highest pathologic stage** in one procedure, biopsy a suspected distant metastasis or N2/N3 mediastinal node (via **EBUS-TBNA**) rather than the primary lung mass.
> - **NEVER start PD-1/PD-L1 Immunotherapy (Pembrolizumab) BEFORE molecular driver results return** in advanced Adenocarcinoma: Even if PD-L1 is $\ge 50\%$, patients with an **EGFR or ALK mutation** do _not_ respond to immunotherapy and develop life-threatening **immune-mediated pneumonitis** if subsequently switched to Osimertinib or Alectinib.
> - **Classic Paraneoplastic Associations:**
>   - **Squamous Cell:** **Hypercalcemia** via **PTHrP** (osteolytic-independent; suppressed intact PTH).
>   - **Small Cell (SCLC):** **SIADH** (euvolemic hyponatremia via ADH), **Cushing Syndrome** (ectopic ACTH $\rightarrow$ hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis, weight loss, hyperpigmentation), and **Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome (LEMS)** (anti-P/Q-type VGCC antibodies $\rightarrow$ proximal weakness that _improves_ with repeated muscle use).

> [!attention]- Clinical Presentation
> **History**
>
> - Often **asymptomatic** until advanced; early detection relies on incidental imaging or LDCT screening.
> - **Intrathoracic symptoms:** **Persistent cough (>3–8 weeks)** or change in chronic smoker's cough, **hemoptysis**, dyspnea, chest pain, or recurrent post-obstructive pneumonia in the same lobe.
> - **Constitutional & Metastatic symptoms:** Unexplained weight loss, anorexia; **bone pain** (vertebral/rib metastases), **headache/seizures/ataxia** (**Brain**), jaundice/RUQ pain (**Liver**), or flank pain (**Adrenal glands**—most common site per gram of tissue).
> - **Adenocarcinoma:** Most common subtype in **never-smokers, women, and younger patients** (peripherally located).
>
> **Physical**
>
> - **Superior Vena Cava (SVC) Syndrome (SCLC > Squamous):** Facial/neck/upper extremity plethora and edema, **non-pulsatile jugular venous distension**, dilated collateral chest wall veins, and dyspnea/headache worse when bending forward (**Pemberton's sign**).
> - **Pancoast Tumor (Superior Sulcus Tumor; Squamous > Adeno):**
>   - **Shoulder/scapular pain** radiating down the ulnar forearm (C8–T2 brachial plexus invasion) + intrinsic hand muscle wasting (**Klumpke palsy**).
>   - **Ipsilateral Horner Syndrome** (sympathetic chain invasion: **P**tosis, **A**nhidrosis, **M**iosis).
> - **Hoarseness** (Left recurrent laryngeal nerve compression at the aortopulmonary window), **elevated hemidiaphragm** (Phrenic nerve), or **Digital Clubbing / Hypertrophic Pulmonary Osteoarthropathy (HPOA)** (Adenocarcinoma/NSCLC; painful periostitis of wrists/ankles).

> [!check]- Diagnostic Criteria
>
> - **Mandatory Tissue Biopsy Confirmation:**
>   - Histopathologic and immunohistochemical (IHC) confirmation from the primary tumor, lymph node, or metastasis (**NEVER diagnose or treat lung cancer based on imaging or sputum cytology alone**).
>   - **Key IHC Markers:**
>     - **Adenocarcinoma:** **TTF-1 (+)** and **Napsin A (+)** (histology shows gland formation/mucin; lepidic growth in adenocarcinoma _in situ_).
>     - **Squamous Cell Carcinoma:** **p40 (+)**, **p63 (+)**, and **CK5/6 (+)** (histology shows **keratin pearls** and **intercellular bridges**).
>     - **Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC):** **Synaptophysin (+)**, **Chromogranin (+)**, **CD56 (+)**, **TTF-1 (+)**, and **Ki-67 proliferation index >70–90%** (small dark blue cells with scant cytoplasm, nuclear molding, and **Azzopardi effect**).
> - **Mandatory Molecular Biomarker Panel (All Advanced/Resectable Nonsquamous NSCLC):**
>   - Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) for **EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF V600E, KRAS G12C, MET exon 14 skipping, RET, NTRK, HER2** PLUS **PD-L1 IHC (TPS %)**.

> [!fail]- Further Investigations
> **Labs**
>
> - **CBC, CMP (Calcium, Na+, Alk Phos, LFTs, Cr), LDH:** Screen for hypercalcemia (Squamous/bone mets), hyponatremia (SIADH in SCLC), and liver/bone involvement.
>
> **Imaging (Staging Workup)**
>
> - **Contrast-Enhanced CT Chest & Upper Abdomen (including Liver and Adrenals):** First-line mandatory test for all suspected lung cancers.
> - **Whole-Body FDG PET-CT (Skull Base to Mid-Thigh):** Mandatory for staging all Stage I–III NSCLC and Limited-Stage SCLC to detect occult nodal or distant metastases (EXCEPT for brain, which has high physiological glucose uptake).
> - **Contrast-Enhanced Brain MRI:** Mandatory in **all SCLC patients** (high rate of occult brain mets) and **Stage II–IV NSCLC** (or any patient with neurologic symptoms).
>
> **Tissue Sampling Modality Selection**
>
> - **Central Lesion OR Mediastinal/Hilar Nodes (N1/N2/N3):** **Flexible Bronchoscopy with Endobronchial Ultrasound-Guided Transbronchial Needle Aspiration (EBUS-TBNA)**.
> - **Peripheral Pulmonary Mass/Nodule:** **CT-Guided Transthoracic Core Needle Biopsy** or Electromagnetic Navigational/Robotic Bronchoscopy.
> - **Pleural Effusion or Supraclavicular Node:** **Ultrasound-Guided Thoracentesis** (2 taps yield ~75–80%) or **Supraclavicular Lymph Node Biopsy** (least invasive way to prove Stage IIIB/IV).
> - **Pre-Operative Physiologic Assessment (Before Lung Resection):** Measure **Pre-op FEV1 and DLCO**: If both are **>80% predicted**, patient is cleared for lobectomy/pneumonectomy; if either is **<80%**, calculate **Predicted Post-Operative (PPO) FEV1 & DLCO** (if PPO <30–40%, perform **Cardiopulmonary Exercise Testing [CPET / $\dot{V}\text{O}_{2\text{max}}$]**; $\dot{V}\text{O}_{2\text{max}} <10$ mL/kg/min contraindicates major resection).

> [!bug]- Red Flags
>
> - **SVC Syndrome WITH Stridor / Laryngeal Edema OR Coma (Cerebral Edema)** $\rightarrow$ True oncologic airway/neurologic emergency; elevate head of bed, secure airway, give IV corticosteroids/loop diuretics, and perform **emergent Endovascular SVC Stenting** (do NOT radiate before obtaining a tissue biopsy unless unstable!).
> - **New back pain + lower extremity weakness, sensory level, or urinary retention** $\rightarrow$ **Malignant Epidural Spinal Cord Compression**; administer **Stat IV Dexamethasone (10 mg bolus)**, order emergent **Whole-Spine MRI**, and consult Neurosurgery/Radiation Oncology.
> - **Acute confusion, vomiting, constipation, shortened QTc, and Ca$^{2+}$ >14 mg/dL** $\rightarrow$ **Hypercalcemia of Malignancy**; treat with **aggressive IV 0.9% Normal Saline (200–300 mL/hr) + IV Zoledronic Acid (4 mg) + Calcitonin**.

> [!todo]- Differentials
> **Life-Threatening & Can't Miss**
>
> - **Pulmonary Metastasis from Extrathoracic Primary:** Breast, colon, renal cell, melanoma, or sarcoma (typically multiple bilateral rounded "cannonball" nodules).
> - **Pulmonary Tuberculosis or Nontuberculous Mycobacteria:** Apical cavitary lesion, night sweats, weight loss, positive AFB/GeneXpert.
> - **Lymphoma:** Anterior mediastinal mass, B-symptoms, diffuse adenopathy.
>
> **Most Likely (Solitary Pulmonary Nodule Mimics)**
>
> - **Infectious Granuloma (Histoplasmosis, Coccidioidomycosis, healed TB):** Benign central, laminated, or diffuse calcification.
> - **Pulmonary Hamartoma:** Most common benign lung tumor; classic **"popcorn" calcification** and intralesional fat on CT.
>
> **Most Surprising**
>
> - **Bronchial Carcinoid Tumor:** Young non-smoker with recurrent focal wheezing, hemoptysis, or post-obstructive pneumonia from a vascular pink endobronchial mass.
>
> **Less Common**
>
> - **Granulomatosis with Polyangiitis (GPA) / Rheumatoid Nodules / Sarcoidosis**

> [!summary]- Management
> **Treatment Goals**
>
> - **Primary Target:** Achieve **complete surgical cure (R0 resection)** or definitive chemoradiation cure in early/locally advanced disease (Stages I–III NSCLC & Limited-Stage SCLC), and **prolong overall survival while preserving quality of life** in metastatic disease (Stage IV).
> - **Secondary Target:** Rapidly relieve airway obstruction, SVC compression, or painful bone/brain metastases.
>
> **Immediate / Emergencies**
>
> - **Perform Endovascular Superior Vena Cava Stenting** for life-threatening SVC Syndrome (stridor, hemodynamic compromise, cerebral edema), while obtaining tissue biopsy prior to starting systemic chemotherapy (for SCLC/lymphoma) or radiation (for NSCLC).
> - **Administer IV Dexamethasone (10 mg IV STAT, then 4 mg q6h) and arrange Emergent Decompressive Surgery + Radiation** for Malignant Spinal Cord Compression or symptomatic Brain Metastases with mass effect.
>
> **Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) — Stage-Directed Therapy**
>
> - **Stage I & II (T1–T2, N0–N1, M0):**
>   - **Perform Surgical Lobectomy (or anatomical segmentectomy for peripheral T1a $\le 2$ cm) with Mediastinal Lymph Node Dissection** as first-line curative therapy.
>   - **Administer Stereotactic Body Radiation Therapy (SBRT / SABR)** as the definitive curative alternative in **medically inoperable** Stage I/II patients (e.g., severe COPD/CHF).
>   - **Add Adjuvant Systemic Therapy for Stage IB (high-risk) to Stage IIIA:** Give **Adjuvant Platinum-Doublet Chemotherapy**, followed by **Adjuvant Osimertinib (for 3 years if EGFR exon 19 del / L858R+)**, **Adjuvant Alectinib (for 2 years if ALK+)**, or **Perioperative/Neoadjuvant Chemo-Immunotherapy (Nivolumab/Pembrolizumab)** if EGFR/ALK wild-type.
> - **Stage III (Unresectable Locally Advanced; N2/N3 Mediastinal Nodes):**
>   - **Administer Definitive Concurrent Platinum-Based Chemoradiation followed by 1 Year of Consolidation Durvalumab (Anti-PD-L1; PACIFIC regimen)**—or **Consolidation Osimertinib (LAURA trial)** if EGFR-mutated—to significantly prolong progression-free and overall survival.
> - **Stage IV (Metastatic NSCLC — Personalized by Biomarker):**
>   - **Actionable Driver Mutation Present (1st-Line Targeted Oral TKI regardless of PD-L1%):**
>     - **EGFR (Exon 19 deletion or Exon 21 L858R):** **Osimertinib** (3rd-gen EGFR TKI +/- chemotherapy or Lazertinib+Amivantamab).
>     - **ALK Rearrangement:** **Lorlatinib**, **Alectinib**, or **Brigatinib**.
>     - **ROS1 Rearrangement:** **Entrectinib**, **Crizotinib**, or **Repotrectinib**.
>     - **BRAF V600E:** **Dabrafenib + Trametinib**.
>     - **KRAS G12C (2nd-line):** **Sotorasib** or **Adagrasib**.
>   - **No Driver Mutation & PD-L1 TPS $\ge 50\%$:** **Pembrolizumab, Cemiplimab, or Atezolizumab Monotherapy** (+/- Platinum-doublet chemo).
>   - **No Driver Mutation & PD-L1 TPS <50%:** **Pembrolizumab PLUS Platinum-Doublet Chemotherapy** (Carboplatin/Cisplatin + **Pemetrexed** for Adenocarcinoma; Carboplatin + **Paclitaxel** for Squamous Cell).
>
> **Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC) — Stage-Directed Therapy**
> _(Note: SCLC is already micrometastatic at presentation; **surgery is virtually NEVER indicated** except for rare T1–2N0M0 solitary nodules)._
>
> - **Limited-Stage SCLC (Confined to ipsilateral hemithorax within a single radiation port; ~30%):**
>   - **Administer Concurrent Cisplatin (or Carboplatin) + Etoposide WITH Thoracic Radiation Therapy**, followed by **Consolidation Durvalumab (for up to 2 years — ADRIATIC trial, 2024)** and **Prophylactic Cranial Irradiation (PCI)** in responders to prevent brain metastases and improve survival.
> - **Extensive-Stage SCLC (Metastatic beyond one hemithorax or malignant effusion; ~70%):**
>   - **Administer Carboplatin (or Cisplatin) + Etoposide PLUS an Anti-PD-L1 Checkpoint Inhibitor (Atezolizumab or Durvalumab)** for 4 cycles, followed by maintenance immunotherapy.
>
> **Palliative Care & Follow-up**
>
> - **Integrate Early Outpatient Specialist Palliative Care** at the time of diagnosis for all patients with Stage IV NSCLC or Extensive-Stage SCLC (proven in landmark RCTs to **improve quality of life, reduce depression, and prolong overall survival**).
> - **Perform Surveillance Chest CT every 6 months for 2–3 years, then annually** in patients who have completed curative-intent therapy for Stage I–III NSCLC.

> [!warning]- Contraindications & Pitfalls
>
> - **Avoid Bevacizumab (Anti-VEGF monoclonal antibody) in Squamous Cell Carcinoma or any patient with hemoptysis** due to a high risk of **fatal cavitary pulmonary hemorrhage**.
> - **Avoid Pemetrexed (antifolate chemotherapy) in Squamous Cell Carcinoma**; high thymidylate synthase expression in squamous tumors renders pemetrexed ineffective (always supplement Pemetrexed with **Folic Acid + Vitamin B12** in Adenocarcinoma to prevent severe myelosuppression).
> - **Avoid initiating Emergency Radiation Therapy for SVC Syndrome BEFORE obtaining a tissue biopsy** (unless acute airway obstruction/coma is present); pre-biopsy radiation causes tumor necrosis and obscures the histology in up to 40% of cases.
> - **Avoid surgical resection in Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)** (unless confirmed Stage I T1-2N0M0 via PET and EBUS); SCLC is a systemic disease requiring chemotherapy + immunotherapy/radiation.
> - **Avoid Beta-Carotene supplementation in current or former smokers**; clinical trials (CARET/ATBC) proved that beta-carotene **increases lung cancer incidence and mortality**.

> [!error]- Complications
>
> - **Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor (PD-1/PD-L1) Toxicities (irAEs)** $\rightarrow$ **Immune-Mediated Pneumonitis** (new cough, hypoxia, ground-glass infiltrates), colitis, hepatitis, thyroiditis, or hypophysitis $\rightarrow$ Hold immunotherapy immediately and start **High-Dose Systemic Corticosteroids (Prednisone 1–2 mg/kg/day)** for Grade $\ge 2$ pneumonitis.
> - **Superior Vena Cava (SVC) Syndrome** $\rightarrow$ Facial/arm swelling, distended neck/chest veins, dyspnea $\rightarrow$ Endovascular stenting + histology-guided chemo/radiation.
> - **SIADH (in SCLC)** $\rightarrow$ Euvolemic hyponatremia (Na+ <130 mEq/L, serum osmolality <275, urine osmolality >100, urine Na+ >30) $\rightarrow$ Treat underlying SCLC, **Fluid Restriction (<800–1,000 mL/day)**, salt tablets + loop diuretics, or **Hypertonic 3% Saline** if seizing/severely symptomatic.

> [!success]- Typical Scenario
> A 63-year-old female never-smoker presents with a 2-month history of dry cough and a 10-lb unintentional weight loss; Chest X-Ray and subsequent CT Chest reveal a 3.8 cm spiculated right upper lobe peripheral mass, ipsilateral mediastinal lymphadenopathy, and two lytic vertebral lesions. EBUS-guided biopsy of a mediastinal lymph node confirms **TTF-1 and Napsin-A positive Lung Adenocarcinoma (Stage IVA)**, Brain MRI is negative, and Next-Generation Sequencing reveals an **EGFR Exon 19 deletion** with PD-L1 expression of 60%. Avoiding PD-1 immunotherapy despite the high PD-L1 score, you initiate first-line targeted oral therapy with **Osimertinib**, start **zoledronic acid** for her bone metastases, and arrange early concurrent outpatient palliative care.
