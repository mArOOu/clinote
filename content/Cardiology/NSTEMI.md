> [!abstract] NSTEMI (Non-ST-Elevation Myocardial Infarction)
> Acute myocardial ischemic necrosis characterized by elevated cardiac biomarkers (troponin) in the clinical setting of acute coronary syndrome (ACS), lacking acute ST-segment elevation on ECG.

> [!note] Pathophysiology
>
> - Typically erosion or rupture of an atherosclerotic plaque leading to non-occlusive intraluminal thrombus and distal embolization.
> - Results in subendocardial ischemia and eventual myocyte necrosis due to profound supply/demand mismatch.

> [!Tip]- Clinical PEARLS
>
> - NSTEMI is differentiated from Unstable Angina **only** by the presence of elevated cardiac biomarkers (troponin); initial management at presentation is identical.
> - A normal initial ECG does NOT rule out NSTEMI; serial ECGs (e.g., q15-30m during active pain) are crucial to detect dynamic ischemic changes.
> - Elderly, diabetic, and female patients frequently present atypically (e.g., dyspnea, profound fatigue, nausea, weakness without overt chest pain).
> - Do not delay antiplatelet/anticoagulation therapy while waiting for the initial troponin result if clinical suspicion for ACS is high.

> [!attention]- Clinical Presentation
> **History**
>
> - Retro-sternal chest pressure/tightness occurring at rest or minimal exertion, often >20 mins.
> - Radiation to left arm, neck, jaw, or back.
> - Associated dyspnea, diaphoresis, nausea/vomiting.
>
> **Physical**
>
> - Often unrevealing.
> - Signs of hemodynamic compromise (hypotension, tachycardia, poor perfusion).
> - Signs of acute heart failure (JVD, rales, S3 gallop).
> - New systolic murmur may indicate ischemic mitral regurgitation.

> [!check]- Diagnostic Criteria
>
> - **Rise and/or fall of cardiac troponin (cTn)** with at least one value above the 99th percentile upper reference limit (URL).
>
> **Single Value $>99\text{th}$ Percentile** = Cardiac Injury (Location/Time Unknown)
> **Value $>99\text{th}$ Percentile + Dynamic Delta on Serial Draw + Ischemic Symptoms** = **NSTEMI**
>
> - **PLUS evidence of acute ischemia:** Symptoms of ischemia OR new ischemic ECG changes (ST-depression or deep T-wave inversion).

> [!fail]- Further Investigations
> **Initial (STAT)**
>
> - **12-lead ECG:** Immediately on arrival; repeat with any symptom change.
> - **High-Sensitivity Troponin (hs-cTn):** Baseline and repeat (at 1, 2, or 3 hours per local protocol) to define delta.
>   Let's stick to the most critical ones.
> - **CBC, BMP, Coags (PT/PTT):** Baseline evaluation prior to anticoagulation/catheterization.
>
> **Risk Stratification & Structural**
>
> - **Transthoracic Echocardiogram:** Evaluate regional wall motion abnormalities and EF (usually within 24h).
> - **TIMI or GRACE Risk Score:** Calculate early to guide invasive strategy timing.

> [!bug]- Red Flags
>
> - Refractory or recurrent chest pain despite medical therapy.
> - Hemodynamic instability or cardiogenic shock.
> - Life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias (VT/VF).
> - Acute heart failure/pulmonary edema.

> [!todo]- Differentials
> **Life-Threatening & Can't Miss**
>
> - STEMI (ruled out by ECG)
> - Aortic Dissection
> - Pulmonary Embolism
> - Tension Pneumothorax
>
> **Most Likely**
>
> - Unstable Angina
> - Stable Angina
> - GERD/Esophageal spasm
> - Musculoskeletal chest pain

> [!summary]- Management
> **Treatment Goals**
>
> - **Primary Target:** Immediate relief of ischemia and prevention of MI expansion/reinfarction.
> - **Secondary Target:** Stabilization of atherosclerotic plaque and prevention of long-term MACE/mortality.
>
> **Immediate / Emergencies (Ischemia Relief)**
>
> - **Chewable Aspirin (162–325 mg STAT)** immediately upon suspicion to inhibit platelet cyclooxygenase (if no anaphylaxis history).
> - **Sublingual Nitroglycerin (0.4 mg q5min x3)** for active pain to reduce preload/afterload (ensure SBP >90 mmHg and no recent PDE-5 inhibitor use).
> - **Supplemental Oxygen** only if SpO2 <90% or respiratory distress to correct hypoxemia.
> - **IV Morphine (1–5 mg)** may be considered for severe pain refractory to nitrates (use cautiously as it may delay P2Y12 inhibitor absorption).
>
> **Early Targeted Therapy (DAPT & Anticoagulation)**
>
> - **P2Y12 Inhibitor Loading Dose** ASAP to establish Dual Antiplatelet Therapy (DAPT). Preferred: Ticagrelor (180 mg load) or Prasugrel (60 mg load, if angio planned and no prior stroke/TIA); Alternative: Clopidogrel (600 mg load).
> - **Therapeutic Anticoagulation Initiation** immediately to prevent thrombus propagation. Options: IV Unfractionated Heparin (preferred if catheterization imminent) or SQ Enoxaparin.
>
> **Stabilization & Risk Stratification**
>
> - **High-Intensity Statin (e.g., Atorvastatin 80 mg)** started early (within 24h) for pleiotropic effects and plaque stabilization.
> - **Oral Beta-Blocker (e.g., Metoprolol tartrate)** initiated within 24 hours in hemodynamically stable patients to reduce myocardial oxygen demand.
>
> **Invasive Strategy & Disposition**
>
> - **Immediate Invasive Strategy (<2h)** if refractory angina, hemodynamic instability, or electrical storm.
> - **Early Invasive Strategy (within 24h)** for high-risk patients (GRACE >140, dynamic ECG changes, elevated troponin).
> - **Admit to CCU/Telemetry** for continuous rhythm monitoring and serial assessment.

> [!warning]- Contraindications & Pitfalls
>
> - **Avoid NSAIDs (non-aspirin)** in acute ACS as they increase risk of MACE and myocardial rupture.
> - **Avoid Nitrates** if phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE-5) inhibitors (e.g., sildenafil) used within previous 24–48h to prevent profound hypotension.
> - **Avoid Beta-Blockers** in acute heart failure, low-output state, or risk of cardiogenic shock (e.g., Age >70, SBP <120, HR >110) to prevent decompensation.

> [!error]- Complications
>
> - Cardiogenic shock
> - Acute Heart Failure / Pulmonary Edema
> - Arrhythmias (VT/VF, Atrial fibrillation, high-grade blocks)
> - Recurrent infarction

> [!success]- Typical Scenario
> A 65-year-old male with hypertension and hyperlipidemia presents with 45 minutes of crushing substernal chest pressure radiating to the left jaw and diaphoresis. Initial ECG shows 1mm ST-depression in V4-V6. Initial troponin is elevated above the 99th percentile. He is given chewable aspirin, loaded with ticagrelor, started on a heparin drip, and admitted to the CCU for early coronary angiography within 24 hours.
