> [!abstract] Constipation & Diarrhea
> **Diarrhea:** Passage of **≥3 loose/watery stools per day** (Bristol Stool Scale 6–7) or stool weight **>200 g/day**, classified by duration as **Acute (<14 days)** (90% infectious), **Persistent (14–30 days)**, or **Chronic (>4 weeks)** (mostly non-infectious: osmotic, secretory, inflammatory, or steatorrheal).
> **Constipation:** Infrequent (<3/week) or difficult passage of hard stools (Bristol 1–2), straining, or incomplete evacuation, classified into **Normal-Transit**, **Slow-Transit**, or **Defecatory Disorders (Dyssynergic Defecation)** (after excluding secondary/structural causes).

> [!note] Pathophysiology
>
> - **Stool Osmotic Gap $= 290 - 2 \times (\text{Stool Na}^+ + \text{Stool K}^+)$:**
>   - **Osmotic Diarrhea (Gap >100 mOsm/kg):** Unabsorbed intraluminal solutes (lactose, magnesium, PEG) pull water into the bowel $\rightarrow$ **stops with fasting**.
>   - **Secretory Diarrhea (Gap <50 mOsm/kg):** Active chloride/water secretion or inhibited $\text{Na}^+$ absorption (enterotoxins, VIPoma, carcinoid, bile acids, microscopic colitis) $\rightarrow$ large-volume (>1 L/day) watery diarrhea that **persists during fasting** and wakes the patient at night.
> - **Dyssynergic Defecation:** Paradoxical contraction or failure to relax the **puborectalis muscle** and external anal sphincter during bearing down.

> [!Tip]- Clinical PEARLS
>
> - **Normal-looking colonoscopy with chronic watery diarrhea?** Always take **random colonic biopsies** from the right and left colon to rule out **Microscopic Colitis** (collagenous or lymphocytic colitis), especially in older women taking **NSAIDs, SSRIs, or PPIs**.
> - **Suspect Pelvic Floor Dyssynergia if laxatives fail:** If chronic constipation fails OTC osmotic/stimulant laxatives (or patient uses digital maneuvers/splinting), order **Anorectal Manometry + Balloon Expulsion Test** _before_ stacking expensive prescription secretagogues; **Biofeedback therapy** is the only curative treatment.
> - **Never give loperamide or antibiotics in EHEC (STEC O157:H7):** In afebrile bloody diarrhea, antimotility agents and antibiotics increase Shiga toxin release/absorption and precipitate **Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS)**.
> - **Overflow "paradoxical" diarrhea:** Liquid stool leaking around a hard fecal impaction in elderly/bedbound or opioid-treated patients; **always perform a Digital Rectal Exam (DRE)** before giving antidiarrheals!

> [!attention]- Clinical Presentation
> **History**
>
> - **Diarrhea Localization & Clues:**
>   - _Small-bowel:_ Large-volume, watery or greasy/foul-smelling (steatorrhea), periumbilical cramping, weight loss, nutrient deficiencies.
>   - _Colonic:_ Small-volume, frequent stools, **tenesmus**, urgency, LLQ cramping, visible blood/mucus.
>   - _Nocturnal diarrhea:_ **Always organic** (secretory, IBD, microscopic colitis, diabetic autonomic neuropathy)—**never** IBS.
>   - _Post-cholecystectomy or ileal resection:_ **Bile Acid Malabsorption** (unabsorbed bile salts stimulate colonic water secretion).
> - **Constipation Clues:**
>   - _Defecatory disorder (Pelvic floor dysfunction):_ Prolonged straining, feeling of anal blockage, need for **perineal pressure/vaginal splinting or digital disimpaction**, inability to evacuate even soft stools.
>   - _Culprit medications:_ **Opioids**, **calcium channel blockers** (verapamil), **anticholinergics** (TCAs, antihistamines, antipsychotics), **oral iron**, calcium/aluminum antacids, GLP-1 agonists.
>
> **Physical**
>
> - **Volume depletion signs (Diarrhea):** Orthostatic hypotension, tachycardia, dry mucous membranes, decreased skin turgor.
> - **Specialized Digital Rectal Exam (DRE) (Mandatory in Constipation):**
>   - Inspect perineum for fissures, hemorrhoids, rectal prolapse, or excessive/absent perineal descent.
>   - Assess resting anal tone, palpable mass, or hard **fecal impaction** in the rectal vault.
>   - **Simulated defecation ("push" maneuver):** Ask patient to bear down during DRE $\rightarrow$ failure of external sphincter/puborectalis to relax (or paradoxical tightening) strongly suggests **Dyssynergic Defecation**.

> [!check]- Diagnostic Criteria
>
> - **Chronic Diarrhea (Mechanistic Confirmation):**
>   - **Osmotic vs. Secretory:** Stool Osmotic Gap **>100 mOsm/kg** (Osmotic) vs. **<50 mOsm/kg** (Secretory).
>   - **Inflammatory:** Elevated **Fecal Calprotectin (>50–100 µg/g)** or **Fecal Lactoferrin**.
>   - **Steatorrhea (Fat Malabsorption):** **72-hour Quantitative Fecal Fat >7 g/day** (gold standard) or positive Sudan III stain; **Fecal Elastase-1 <200 µg/g** confirms exocrine pancreatic insufficiency.
> - **Functional Constipation / Chronic Idiopathic Constipation (Rome IV Criteria):**
>   - **≥2 of the following** for ≥25% of defecations (onset ≥6 months ago, active for past 3 months, insufficient criteria for IBS-C): (1) Straining, (2) Lumpy/hard stools (Bristol 1–2), (3) Sensation of incomplete evacuation, (4) Sensation of anorectal obstruction/blockage, (5) Manual maneuvers to facilitate defecation, (6) **<3 spontaneous bowel movements per week**.
> - **Defecatory Disorder (Dyssynergic Defecation):** Meets criteria for functional constipation **PLUS** impaired evacuation demonstrated on **≥2 of 3 physiological tests**: (1) **Abnormal Balloon Expulsion Test** (inability to expel a 50-mL water-filled balloon within 1–2 minutes), (2) **Anorectal Manometry** showing dyssynergia, or (3) Impaired evacuation on **Barium/MR Defecography**.

> [!fail]- Further Investigations
> **Labs**
>
> - **Acute Diarrhea:** **NO stool testing required** for mild, watery, self-limiting diarrhea (<7 days). Order **Stool Multiplex PCR / Culture** (_Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter, Yersinia, Shiga toxin/EHEC_) ONLY if: **dysentery (bloody stools)**, **fever ≥38.5°C**, **severe hypovolemia**, **duration >7 days**, **immunocompromised**, or **inflammatory bowel disease flare**.
>   - _Order \*\*C. difficile_ toxin/PCR:\*\* Recent antibiotics (within 3 months), hospitalization, or PPI use.
>   - _Order Stool Ova & Parasites / Giardia antigen:_ Persistent diarrhea **>14 days**, hikers/well-water, daycare, or MSM.
> - **Chronic Diarrhea Initial Panel:** CBC, CMP, TSH, CRP/ESR, **Celiac serology (IgA anti-tTG + Total IgA)**, **Fecal Calprotectin**, **Giardia antigen**, and **Stool Electrolytes ($\text{Na}^+, \text{K}^+$) + Stool pH** (<5.5 indicates carbohydrate malabsorption/lactose intolerance).
> - **Chronic Constipation Initial Panel:** CBC, **TSH** (hypothyroidism), **Serum Calcium** (hypercalcemia), glucose/HbA1c, potassium (hypokalemia).
>
> **Imaging & Procedures**
>
> - **Colonoscopy with Biopsies:**
>   - _In Constipation:_ Perform **ONLY** if **alarm features** are present OR patient is **due for routine age-appropriate colorectal cancer screening (≥45 years)**.
>   - _In Chronic Diarrhea:_ Indicated for inflammatory diarrhea (high calprotectin) or unexplained chronic watery diarrhea (**must include random biopsies of right/left colon to diagnose Microscopic Colitis** and **terminal ileal intubation** for Crohn's).
> - **Anorectal Manometry + Balloon Expulsion Test:** First-line physiological test when chronic constipation fails initial fiber and osmotic laxative therapy.
> - **Radiopaque Marker (Sitzmark) Study / Wireless Motility Capsule:** Order to confirm **Slow-Transit Constipation (Colonic Inertia)** _only after_ dyssynergic defecation has been ruled out or treated.

> [!bug]- Red Flags
> **Alarm Features (Mandate Prompt Colonoscopy / Workup):**
>
> - **Hematochezia / Melena** or **unexplained Iron-Deficiency Anemia**
> - **Unintended weight loss** (>5–10% body weight)
> - **Nocturnal diarrhea** (waking patient from sleep)
> - **New-onset change in bowel habits or constipation at age ≥45–50 years**
> - **Pencil-thin stools** or **acute inability to pass flatus + abdominal distention** (suspect obstructing colorectal malignancy)
> - **Family history of Colorectal Cancer or Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD)**
> - **High fever, tenesmus, or severe abdominal pain**

> [!todo]- Differentials
> **Life-Threatening & Can't Miss**
>
> - **Obstructing Colorectal Adenocarcinoma:** Progressive constipation, narrowing stool caliber, iron-deficiency anemia, or paradoxical overflow diarrhea in patients ≥45 years.
> - **_Clostridioides difficile_ Colitis (Fulminant):** Profuse watery diarrhea, leukocytosis (>15,000/µL), AKI, toxic megacolon.
> - **Enterohemorrhagic _E. coli_ (EHEC O157:H7):** Afebrile bloody diarrhea; high risk of **Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS)** (microangiopathic hemolytic anemia, thrombocytopenia, AKI).
> - **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (Ulcerative Colitis / Crohn's Disease):** Chronic bloody diarrhea, elevated fecal calprotectin, weight loss, extraintestinal manifestations.
>
> **Most Likely (Chronic Diarrhea by Mechanism)**
>
> - **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS-D or IBS-C):** Abdominal pain related to defecation for >6 months with normal calprotectin, normal celiac serology, and absence of nocturnal symptoms/weight loss.
> - **Osmotic Diarrhea:** **Lactase deficiency** (bloating, flatulence, stool pH <5.5), sugar alcohols (sorbitol, mannitol, xylitol), or surreptitious **Magnesium laxative abuse**.
> - **Secretory Diarrhea:**
>   - **Microscopic Colitis (Collagenous vs. Lymphocytic):** Middle-aged/elderly women with painless watery diarrhea (often nocturnal); **normal endoscopic mucosa**; biopsy shows **thickened subepithelial collagen band ≥10 µm** (collagenous) or **intraepithelial lymphocytes ≥20 per 100 surface epithelial cells** (lymphocytic).
>   - **Bile Acid Diarrhea:** Cholerheic diarrhea following cholecystectomy or terminal ileal resection/Crohn's (<100 cm).
> - **Steatorrhea (Malabsorptive):** **Celiac Disease** (villous atrophy), **Chronic Pancreatitis / Exocrine Pancreatic Insufficiency** (low fecal elastase), **Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO)** (diagnose via glucose/lactulose hydrogen breath test).
>
> **Most Surprising (Neuroendocrine Causes of Secretory Diarrhea)**
>
> - **VIPoma (WDHA Syndrome / Verner-Morrison):** **W**atery **D**iarrhea (>3 L/day, tea-colored), **H**ypokalemia, **A**chlorhydria.
> - **Carcinoid Syndrome:** Secretory diarrhea + episodic cutaneous flushing + right-sided valvular heart lesions; elevated **24-hour urine 5-HIAA**.
> - **Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma:** Calcitonin-mediated secretory diarrhea.

> [!summary]- Management
> **Treatment Goals**
>
> - **Primary Target:** Restoration of normal stool frequency and consistency (Bristol Stool Scale 3–4), euvolemia, and normal electrolytes.
> - **Secondary Target:** Cure underlying etiology (infection, celiac, microscopic colitis, dyssynergic defecation) and exclude colorectal neoplasia.
>
> **Immediate / Emergencies**
>
> - **Administer Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS)** (containing glucose + sodium to utilize intact SGLT1 cotransporters) for mild-to-moderate acute dehydrating diarrhea, or **infuse IV Lactated Ringer's / Normal Saline** immediately if the patient has severe hypovolemia, shock, or intractable vomiting.
> - **Perform manual digital disimpaction** (with local lidocaine jelly ± sedation) followed by a **mineral oil or warm water enema** in patients presenting with **acute fecal impaction** or paradoxical overflow diarrhea (after ruling out perforation/obstruction).
>
> **Targeted Management of Acute & Chronic Diarrhea**
>
> - **Administer Loperamide** (4 mg PO initially, then 2 mg after each loose stool; max 16 mg/day) or **Bismuth Subsalicylate** for symptomatic relief of **non-bloody, afebrile acute watery diarrhea** or chronic functional diarrhea.
> - **Prescribe targeted antibiotics (Azithromycin 500 mg PO daily for 3 days or single-dose 1 g)** ONLY for **severe dysentery (fever + bloody stools)**, severe traveler's diarrhea, cholera, or immunocompromised hosts (_Campylobacter_ and _Shigella_ have high fluoroquinolone resistance; **Azithromycin is preferred**).
> - **Stop culprit medications (NSAIDs, SSRIs, PPIs) and initiate Oral Budesonide (9 mg PO daily for 6–8 weeks)** as **first-line therapy for Microscopic Colitis** to induce clinical and histologic remission.
> - **Initiate a Bile Acid Sequestrant (Cholestyramine 4 g PO daily–BID, Colesevelam, or Colestipol)** as empiric or definitive first-line therapy for **Bile Acid Diarrhea** (post-cholecystectomy or post-ileal resection).
> - **Prescribe Rifaximin (550 mg PO TID for 14 days)** for **Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO)** or IBS-D.
>
> **Stepwise Management of Chronic Idiopathic Constipation (CIC) (2023 AGA/ACG Guidelines)**
>
> - **Step 1 (Lifestyle & Fiber):** Increase dietary fluid intake and initiate **soluble fiber supplementation (Psyllium 15–25 g/day)** titrated slowly over weeks for mild normal-transit constipation.
> - **Step 2 (First-Line Osmotic Laxatives):** **Start Polyethylene Glycol (PEG 3350, 17 g PO daily)** (strongest guideline recommendation for long-term daily use) or **Magnesium Oxide** (avoid in CKD) / **Lactulose** if fiber is insufficient or causes bloating.
> - **Step 3 (Short-Term or Rescue Stimulant Laxatives):** Add or use **Senna** or **Bisacodyl (5–10 mg PO)** as rescue therapy or short-term adjuncts to stimulate myenteric plexus peristalsis.
> - **Step 4 (Prescription Secretagogues & Prokinetics for Laxative-Refractory CIC):**
>   - **Initiate Linaclotide (72–290 mcg PO daily, 30 min before breakfast)** or **Plecanatide (3 mg PO daily)** (Guanylate Cyclase-C agonists) or **Lubiprostone (24 mcg PO BID)** (ClC-2 chloride channel activator) to stimulate intestinal chloride and fluid secretion.
>   - **Initiate Prucalopride (1–2 mg PO daily)** (selective $\text{5-HT}_4$ receptor agonist prokinetic) to directly stimulate colonic high-amplitude propagating contractions.
>
> **Specific Constipation Subtypes (Dyssynergia & Opioid-Induced)**
>
> - **Refer for Anorectal Biofeedback Therapy** (pelvic floor physical therapy with manometric visual feedback) as the **definitive first-line treatment for Dyssynergic Defecation** (80% efficacy; laxatives alone will fail).
> - **Prescribe a Peripherally Acting $\mu$-Opioid Receptor Antagonist (PAMORA)**—such as **Naldemedine (0.2 mg PO daily)**, **Naloxegol (25 mg PO daily)**, or **Methylnaltrexone (SC/PO)**—for **Opioid-Induced Constipation (OIC)** that fails standard osmotic/stimulant laxatives, to block gut opioid receptors without crossing the blood-brain barrier or reversing central analgesia.

> [!warning]- Contraindications & Pitfalls
>
> - **Avoid Loperamide/Diphenoxylate AND Antibiotics in suspected Shiga toxin-producing _E. coli_ (STEC / EHEC O157:H7)** (afebrile bloody diarrhea) and **avoid Loperamide alone in fulminant _C. difficile_ or acute severe Ulcerative Colitis** to prevent **Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome (HUS)** and **toxic megacolon**.
> - **Avoid Magnesium-based laxatives (Milk of Magnesia, Magnesium Citrate) and Sodium Phosphate enemas in patients with Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) or Heart Failure** to prevent fatal hypermagnesemia, hyperphosphatemia, and acute phosphate nephropathy.
> - **Avoid Bulk-Forming Fiber (Psyllium) in patients with Opioid-Induced Constipation, Severe Slow-Transit Constipation, or Strictures**, as fiber without adequate motility/water forms a concrete-like **bezoar/fecal impaction** and worsens bloating.
> - **Avoid Docusate (Colace)** for preventing or treating constipation; multiple randomized trials show docusate is **no more effective than placebo** (always use PEG + senna instead for hospitalized/opioid patients).
> - **Avoid Enemas or Rectal Suppositories in patients with severe neutropenia or thrombocytopenia** to prevent mucosal trauma, bacterial translocation, and fatal neutropenic bacteremia.

> [!error]- Complications
>
> - **Stercoral Colitis & Perforation:** Rock-hard fecaloma in the rectosigmoid causes pressure necrosis of the colonic wall $\rightarrow$ focal peritonitis, CT shows impacted stool with pericolic fat stranding/wall thickening $\rightarrow$ high mortality if perforated.
> - **Melanosis Coli:** Benign, reversible brown-black pigmentation of the colonic mucosa caused by **anthraquinone stimulant laxatives (Senna, Cascara)** inducing apoptosis of epithelial cells and lipofuscin uptake by macrophages $\rightarrow$ harmless finding on colonoscopy.
> - **Factitious Diarrhea (Laxative Abuse):** Classic in eating disorders or secondary gain $\rightarrow$ **Hypokalemia + Metabolic Alkalosis** (with chronic volume depletion), **Stool Osmotic Gap >100 mOsm/kg with elevated stool Magnesium (>45 mmol/L)** (if magnesium abuse) or low osmotic gap (if stimulant abuse).
> - **Post-Infectious Irritable Bowel Syndrome (PI-IBS) & Secondary Lactase Deficiency:** Occurs in ~10% of patients following acute bacterial/parasitic gastroenteritis due to transient loss of brush-border lactase or persistent mucosal immune activation $\rightarrow$ temporarily avoid dairy products after acute gastroenteritis.

> [!success]- Typical Scenario
> **Scenario 1 (Chronic Diarrhea):** A 66-year-old woman on daily sertraline and ibuprofen presents with 3 months of 6–8 painless, watery bowel movements per day, including 2 episodes nightly that wake her from sleep; labs (CBC, CMP, TSH, tTG-IgA, fecal calprotectin) are normal. Colonoscopy reveals completely normal-appearing mucosa throughout the colon, but **random colonic biopsies** demonstrate a thickened subepithelial collagen band (>10 µm), confirming **Collagenous (Microscopic) Colitis**; ibuprofen and sertraline are discontinued, and she achieves complete remission with **oral Budesonide 9 mg daily for 8 weeks**.
> **Scenario 2 (Chronic Constipation):** A 38-year-old woman presents with lifelong severe straining, hard stools twice weekly, and a need to press on her perineum to evacuate stool, unresponsive to daily polyethylene glycol and senna. DRE reveals paradoxical contraction of the puborectalis during simulated defecation, and **Anorectal Manometry with a Balloon Expulsion Test (>2 minutes)** confirms **Dyssynergic Defecation**, which resolves with **Anorectal Biofeedback Therapy**.
